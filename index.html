Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

Gemba Lab (“we”, “our”, or “us”) designs professional production software for macOS and iOS. We believe your creative data, footage, and project assets are strictly your own property. Our applications are engineered with a zero-knowledge, privacy-by-design philosophy.

1. Zero Data Collection on Footage & Media

None of our applications (including Offloader Pro, MediaCheck, HyperCatalog, LumaScope Pro, LumaLUT, LumaMatch, CueFlow Studio, SlateFlow, ScriptFlow, or CartPad) upload, transmit, or inspect the contents of your video clips, audio tracks, camera cards, or look-up tables. All file operations, checksum hashing, and color rendering take place entirely locally on your hardware.

2. Local Network Communication

Telemetry connections between macOS parent apps and iOS companion apps (such as Offloader Companion or CueFlow Stage) operate primarily over peer-to-peer local network protocols. Only essential status telemetry (e.g. transfer percentages, ETA, timecode indicators) is transmitted across the local link. No media files or raw footage are ever routed through external cloud servers.

3. Analytics & Crash Reporting

We do not use third-party user-tracking trackers or ad SDKs. Anonymous crash logs and diagnostic telemetry may be processed solely through Apple’s native App Store crash reporting services if you have opted in via your Apple Account settings.

4. In-App Purchases

All financial transactions and subscriptions are handled securely through Apple’s StoreKit. Gemba Lab never sees or stores your payment details or credit card information.
